Plan your week, scramble your meals, and never wonder "what's for dinner" again. Join a household code to share a real-time synced plan, then hit Scramble to auto-fill the week from your recipe library — it never repeats last week's dishes and balances proteins across days.

Swap, skip, or lock any slot before the shopping list builds itself, with ingredients merged across recipes and scaled to your servings. Every locked week is saved to history, ready to reuse. A look at every screen of the app.
